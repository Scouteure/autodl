AutoDL

AutoDL is a private, single-user tool. Its owner uses it on his own computer to manage his own YouTube channels: reading channel analytics (views, watch time, retention, impressions) and editing the titles, descriptions, thumbnails, playlists and publication schedule of his own videos, through the official YouTube API Services.

AutoDL is not offered to the public, has no sign-up, and does not access any channel other than the ones its owner authorizes himself.
